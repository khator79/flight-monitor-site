Flight Tracker Bot

A personal, single-user flight tracker. Not a public service.

Flight Tracker Bot helps one family keep track of their own trips. Booking confirmations are forwarded to a dedicated mailbox that exists only for this purpose. The app reads that one mailbox with read-only access, works out which flights are booked, checks their status on the day of travel, and posts short updates to a private family chat.

It does not read anyone's personal mailbox, cannot send, change or delete email, and is not offered to other users.
