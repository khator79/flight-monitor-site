Privacy Policy

Flight Tracker Bot · Last updated 25 November 2026

Flight Tracker Bot is a personal app used by a single person to track their own family's flights. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

The app connects to one dedicated Gmail account, created only to receive forwarded flight booking emails, using Google's read-only Gmail permission (gmail.readonly). It refuses to run against any other account. It cannot send, modify or delete email.

What it does with the data

  • It reads booking emails (and attached PDF boarding passes) to find flight details: flight numbers, dates, times, airports, booking references and traveller names.
  • To read those details, the email text is sent to an AI service (OpenAI) with contact details, payment details and links removed first. The request is made with storage turned off.
  • On the day of travel, flight numbers and dates are looked up with a flight status service. No personal details are sent to it.
  • Short updates are posted to a private family chat on Telegram, with booking references partly hidden.

What it stores

Only the structured flight details listed above, in a private database. Email bodies, attachments and subjects are never stored. Logs never contain booking references, names or email content.

What it never does

  • Sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, or use it for advertising.
  • Use Google user data for anything other than tracking the family's own flights.
  • Use Google user data to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access). The stored flight details can be deleted by the app's owner at any time.
